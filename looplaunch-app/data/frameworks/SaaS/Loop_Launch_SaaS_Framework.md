# Loop Launch SaaS Growth & Retention Framework

## Chapter 1: Acquisition Loops vs Funnels
Traditional funnels lose energy at the bottom; growth loops reinvest output from one cycle to feed the next. 

### Core Loop Mechanics
1. **Viral Loop**: New user signs up -> creates an artifact (report, shareable workspace, invitation) -> exposes new prospect -> prospect signs up.
2. **Paid Performance Reinvestment Loop**: User subscribes -> generates immediate net gross profit -> profit funds customer acquisition cost (CAC) on target ad channels within 30 days.
3. **Content & SEO Compounding Loop**: Product activity generates programmatic indexable pages -> captures long-tail search intent -> converts organic visitors.

## Chapter 2: The 30-Day Payback Heuristic
For B2B SaaS companies targeting seed to series A:
- If CAC payback period exceeds 12 months, growth will stall due to working capital constraints.
- Target CAC payback: 5 to 7 months for SMB ($50-$200/mo ACV), 8 to 11 months for Mid-Market ($500-$2,000/mo ACV).
- Retention is the bedrock: If net revenue retention (NRR) is below 105%, paid acquisition merely accelerates churn.

## Chapter 3: Positioning & ICP Anti-Personas
Positioning is not what you tell the market; it is what the market believes you solve uniquely.
- **The Anti-Persona Principle**: Define explicitly who you DO NOT sell to. Saying "we serve all businesses" dilutes positioning and kills outbound conversion.
- **The High-Velocity Hook**: Identify the single highest-urgency trigger event (e.g., funding round closed, compliance deadline, key competitor price hike) that makes a prospect seek a solution within 48 hours.
